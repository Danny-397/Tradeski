Ski · your financial analyst
Given today's Fed rate decision, what in my holdings is most exposed?
Your growth positions carry the most rate sensitivity. When the Fed holds rates elevated, the discount rate applied to future earnings rises, which compresses valuations on high-multiple names first. NVDA and similar tech holdings reprice harder than defensive names. AAPL is more insulated given its cash position and buyback program, though it's not immune.
What changed for my positions since yesterday?
I can see your current holdings and live P&L. The 1D chart on any position shows where it opened versus where it trades now. Cross-reference that with the news sentiment panel: if a position is down but sentiment is unchanged and macro data is stable, that's more likely noise than a signal worth acting on.
How does current inflation data affect my AAPL and NVDA positions?
The CPI reading in your macro ribbon is the baseline. Elevated inflation keeps the Fed hawkish, which compresses forward earnings multiples. NVDA gets hit harder because its valuation is weighted toward future earnings. If CPI cools toward 2%, both names benefit, but NVDA disproportionately.
